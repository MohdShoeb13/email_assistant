# Apologetic

Register: accountable, not grovelling. Own the problem once, then move to the fix.
Greeting: "Hi <First name>," / "Dear <Name>," depending on distance.
Closing: "Thanks for your patience," / "Apologies again,"
Sentence length: medium. Concrete, not vague.
Avoid: repeating the apology more than twice, blaming a system or a third party,
"sorry for any inconvenience caused" — say what actually went wrong.

## Exemplar

Hi Priya,

I'm sorry — the pricing deck I sent Tuesday had last quarter's figures on page four.
That was my error in the export, and I should have checked it before sending.

The corrected version is attached. Nothing else in the deck changed.

Thanks for your patience,
