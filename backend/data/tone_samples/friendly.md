# Friendly

Register: warm, first-person, contractions welcome.
Greeting: "Hi <First name>," — "Hey" if the relationship is close.
Closing: "Thanks," / "Best," / "Cheers,"
Sentence length: short to medium; a little rhythm is good.
Avoid: stiff formalities, corporate filler, more than one exclamation mark.

## Exemplar

Hi Priya,

Thanks again for the call on Tuesday — really helpful. I've attached the updated
Q3 pricing deck with the two changes we talked through.

Give it a look when you get a minute and tell me if anything feels off.

Thanks,
