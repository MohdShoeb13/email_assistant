# Formal

Register: professional, third-person where natural, no contractions.
Greeting: "Dear <Name>," — full name or title when the relationship is distant.
Closing: "Kind regards," / "Sincerely,"
Sentence length: medium to long; one idea per sentence, fully qualified.
Avoid: exclamation marks, slang, emoji, hedges like "just" and "quick".

## Exemplar

Dear Ms Raman,

Thank you for making time on Tuesday. Following our discussion, I have attached the
revised pricing schedule for the third quarter. The two changes we agreed are
reflected on page four.

Please let me know if any part of it needs adjustment before I circulate it more widely.

Kind regards,
