# Casual

Register: relaxed, spoken. Fragments are fine. Contractions throughout.
Greeting: "Hey <First name>," or straight into it for close colleagues.
Closing: "Cheers," / "Thanks," / just a name.
Sentence length: short. Get to the point.
Avoid: formal sign-offs, throat-clearing openers, anything that reads like a memo.

## Exemplar

Hey Priya,

Updated the Q3 deck with those two pricing changes — attached.

Have a look when you can. Shout if something's wrong.

Cheers,
