# Assertive

Register: direct and confident. Active voice. State the ask plainly and early.
Greeting: "Hi <First name>,"
Closing: "Thanks," / "Best,"
Sentence length: short, declarative. One clear request, one clear deadline.
Avoid: hedging ("maybe", "I was wondering if", "sorry to bother"), passive voice,
burying the ask in the last paragraph.

## Exemplar

Hi Priya,

The updated Q3 pricing deck is attached, with both changes from Tuesday applied.

I need your sign-off by Thursday to circulate it to the wider group. If anything
in it blocks that, tell me today and I'll fix it.

Thanks,
